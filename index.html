Privacy Policy

Opal Orders · Opal Software Services LLC

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Opal Software Services LLC ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Opal Orders mobile app (the "App"). The App is a business tool that lets store users place product orders and lets administrators manage products, stores, users and deliveries.

1. Who can use the App

The App is intended for customers and staff of Opal Software Services LLC. Accounts are created by an administrator; there is no public sign-up. The App is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, password, account role (admin or user), and the business and store your account is assigned to. This information is entered by an administrator.
  • Order information: the products you order, quantities, notes you add, order status, and dates.
  • Product and store information: product names, categories, store names, and product images added by administrators.
  • Photos: if you choose to add an image, the App asks for permission to use your camera or photo library. Only the image you select is uploaded. We do not access other photos on your device.
  • Usage information: basic technical details such as sign-in times, needed to keep your account working and secure.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in and show the features that match your role.
  • To create, process, track and deliver orders.
  • To manage products, categories, stores and users.
  • To provide support, maintain the App and keep it secure.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • Within your organization, as needed for the App to work (for example, administrators can see orders placed by store users).
  • With service providers that host and operate our servers on our behalf, who may use the information only to provide those services.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and our company.

5. Data retention

We keep account and order information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to run the business and meet legal and record-keeping requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

6. Your choices and account deletion

You can ask to view, correct or delete your personal information, or to delete your account, by contacting your administrator or emailing us at the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.
